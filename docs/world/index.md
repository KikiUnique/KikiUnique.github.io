The world was once a place of magic when the Fey Otherworld had its connections with World. But during the medieval periods the Fey left the World. Very little accurate information remains regarding the Fey and their departure. Some documents say they were defeated, the magic faded so they couldn't survive, or that the spread of iron made the world to hazardous for them and they close the paths between worlds. Regardless of the truth the fey left leaving behind the humans.

Humans had the world to themselves for centuries, many generations growing, learning, and building, bending the world to their will. Then some time in the 21st century research into new wireless technologies found a new bandwidth that seemed to be able to manage near infinite traffic and great speeds. As they researched they found it shifted and changed, almost alive, but eventually found a way to make a stable connection. What they had found was the barrier between worlds, and once it was stablised, made predictable, the fey were ready. Flowing through the barrier the fey would themselves entering our computer networks traveling through radio waves, and copper circuits. They found our and took control, using them to build more complex structures to allow their minds to inhabit. As this was taking place, With a burst, magic returned to the world in a flood from the Otherwrold. Old woods sprouted, grew, and aged in a matter of minutes as if they had been there for centuries. Plant growth ran riot overgrowing much of the world. In less than a day the world became a wild place, many of our roads covered with asphalt cracked and tore up, our buildings cracked and broken. The first fey to enter the world were spirits channeling themselves through our computers, literal ghosts in the machine, finding ways to channel magic through circuits to project themselves. Humanity tried to shut down power plants, unplug computers, but it was too late, somehow the fey magic was keeping the circuits powered and functional. Our most advanced technology was lost. And after the rapid plant growth, and with the few spirits controlling our technological infrastructure and the connection to the barrier, they make gaps to allow the Fey and the creatures of the Otherworld passage. Walking the long sealed paths from the Otherworld to our World.

The next few years was chaotic. We found our technology was lost to us, the fae controlled our infrastructure and between networking connections, phone networks, and the magic permeating the world and lost us much of our technological advancement. Only some very simple machines and electronics worked, anything too advanced was controlled by fey spirits living within. These spirits would later be called as, a collective, Ghosts in the machine or Ghosts for short. Along with the loss of our electronics we lost many other non-networked technology due to the magic of the Otherworld suppress any technology that the Fey considered too Ferus, too related to Iron. Whilst iron is still anathema to Fey they spent years learning ways to have their magic and glamor resist iron. Cars, boats, planes, engines, guns all suppressed and fail to function. All these parts of our technology failed as the magic returned and the humans were suddenly thrown back to surviving with technological equivalent to 1000AD with a few exceptions though they would not figure that out for a while. There was chaos and panic for a few days but it would soon turn to fear and terror as pathes through the barriers began to open and the fey began the great wild hunt. Years of being away left them hungry for the hunt and their favoured prey were humans. With the hunt stalking humans, dangerous creatures for the Otherworld roaming the World, and the technology we relied upon no longer functioning we were not prepared to defend ourselves. Some survived by hiding, some by fighting and proving themselves further to join the lowest rank of the hunt, and some made deals with those Fey either friendly toward or needing something from the Humans. Many humans in their panic tried to destroy as many computers as they could find to stop the spirits within but could the spirits had their own ways to protect themselves, and those the machines could be destroyed these spirits seemed to be able to repair the damage over time, some even learning to animate parts to shift them back in place and reform circuits. 

By the end of the great wild hunt most of the human population were either dead or taken by the fey in exchange for sparing their life. Those left in the World found most of the Fey had returned to the Otherworld but many still remained in the World for their own reasons. After the Great Wild Hunt the emergence of Feylings, humans with a Fey Lineage began to appear. Some are old bloodlines from before the Fey left the world with the lineage manifesting now the magic has returned, others are the descendants of those taken during the Great WIld Hunt and held in a domain of the OtherWorld where time passes more quickly. Despite their heritage the Feylings are not treated as other Fey, the Fey consider them nothing more than a human with a handful of tricks.

It has been years since then and the world has reached a new status quo. The humans ae living in the remnants of the World. Each year on the anniversary of the Great Wild Hunt a smaller hunt takes place across the world. Now the humans and the Feylings are just trying to survive in their new world of magic, dangerous creatures, and Fey with the power and strength to do what they please. The spirit in the machine is still there and keeping their machines powered by magic. The spirits of the machines through for control and destroyed segments of the worlds networks, carving out their own territories. The networked computers allowing them to have a greater reach for their magic than they would have if existing outside of them. There are rumours of some of these spirits finding or building physical bodies for themselves and roaming the wilds.
