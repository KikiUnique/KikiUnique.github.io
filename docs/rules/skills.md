The world has become a dangerous place and only the skilled survive. What skills do you have to keep yourself from becoming fodder for the powers that have returned.

## Attributes
Attributes are generic qualities about an individual and are a collection of different aspects about a person. A character starts with the following attribute levels.
- Health (3)
- Stamina (3)
- Willpower (3)

## Skills
Skills are the abilities you have learned along the way. Each skill is a combination of born talent, training, and expertise. Skills are divided into 4 categories, Combative, Crafting, Mental, and Internal.

### Combative
For **Combative** skills you roleplay your attack and anounce you skill level, the target will then apply the attack to damage or armour and should roleplay being hit. This is covered in more detail in the [Combat Rules](/rues/combat) Section.
- Weapon Proficiency \[type\] (1 hand sword, 2 hand sword, 1 hand unbalanced, 2 hand unbalanced, 1 hand blunt, 2 hand blunt, polearm, knife)  
  Weapon proficiency allow you to use the specified type of weapon. When using a weapon you can use it to attack a target using this skills level as the attack value. As the skill levels up you can gain benefits from specially prepared weapons or equipment
    - 3 - You can apply a coating to the weapon to added benefit
    - 5 - Your level is considered +1 when using master crafted weapons (red band weapons)
    - 7 - You have learnt to push beyond your limits and may increase your level for a single strike by up to +2 by using the same amount of stamina
    - 10 - You have mastered this weapon type, if you can acquire an artifact weapon of this type you can use its full potential (see artifacts)
- Shield \[type\] (buckler, shield, tower shield)  
  Shield allows you to use a shield effectively, anyone can hold a shield, but it takes training to use one to defend yourself. Each shield allows you to spend stamina and call Block in response to a visible attack. As you gain great proficiency you gain new skills with a shield.
    - 3 - You can use 1 stamina to call Push Back using your shield on a target within melee range
    - 5 - You can use 2 stamina to attack with your shield using your Shield level and call Push Back
    - 7 - You have learnt to use withstand heavy blows, can spend 1 stamina to call resist when targeted with pushback from a visible target.
    - 10 - You have mastered using Shields of this type, whilst using a shield you again +2 to your armour value.
- Armour \[type\] (Light, Mid, Heavy)  
  Armour allows you to properly put on and take off armour and get the most out of the armour you are wearing. Each type fo armour will list your armour rating based on your skill level. As you level increases you gain new skills with your armour.
    - 3 - You can spend 1 stamina to resist a stun attack (treat as if a standard attack).
    - 5 - You can spend 1 stamina to resist a thru attack (treat as if a standard attack).
    - 7 - You can spend 2 stamina to call disarm using your armor level immediately after being struck.
    - 10 - You have mastered using armour of this type and can spot weak points, you can spend 2 stamina to make a thru call with your weapon.
- First Aid
  First aid allows you to recover health for a target. You can recover health up to your skill level with 1 point returning after 30 seconds of appropriate roleplay. You can not recover health for yourself unless you have an ability that allows it.
  - 3 - You can spend 1 stamina to hastily heal a target which recovers 1 health every 10 seconds but is limited to half your level.
  - 5 - You can spend 1 stamina to heal yourself up to half you skill level so long as you are not bleeding out.
  - 7 - You can spend 1 stamina to heal an additional target at the same time.
  - 10 - Your first aid roleplay time is halved for characters with less than 5 total health.


### Crafting
For **Crafting** skills the level dictates what you can craft in downtime and in session. Crafting is done during downtime to create items with specific effects. These skills may also be used in a similar way to the Mental skills in that they may be required when interacting with some capsules found in the game. For information on crafting see the [Crafting](/rules/crafting) page.
- Alchemy
    - Creation of [Alchemy Items](/rules/crafting/alchemy) such as poisons, acids, and other chemical substances
- Medical
    - Creation of [Medical items](/rules/crafting/medical) such as potions, antidotes, and first aid items
- Arcane Engineer
    - Creation of [Arcane-Device](/rules/crafting/arcne_device) which can replicate some of the effects of Fey magic.
- Smithing
    - Creation of [Armours and Weapons](/rules/crafting/smithing) such as body armour, shields, swords, and axes.
- Old World Engineer
    - Creation of [Devices and Structures](/rules/crafting/engineer) from the old world powered by fey stones.


### Mental
For **Mental** skills the level is used when interacting with items in the world. Some items or areas might have a pack (capsule, enevelope, other...) marked with a code indicating a skill, number indicating a level, and a final number indicating required roleplay duration. For instance Survival, level 3, 5 minutes would be (Sur-03-05). After performing appropriate roleplay for this duration you can open the capsule and read the result. In some cases there may be another capsule inside. First read the contains of the capsule you opened as this will tel you how to handle these.
- Tactics
    - Each level unlocks a new ability
- Factions
    - Knowledge of the factions of the Neverwas keeping up updated with rumours and changes.
- Markets
    - Knowledge or markets, trade. This includes rumours regarding the various markets of the Neverwas
- Otherworld Lore
    - Knowledge of the Otherworld, locations, rules, laws, and shifts in these things
- Survival
    - Knowledge of how to survive in hostile environments, can provide advantages when things turn bad
- World Gaps
    - Knowledge of the various gaps in reality leading to and from the Neverwas


### Internal
For **Internal** skills the level is likely to only be used to gain abilities or benefits as they increase and are checked as a result of some in world event such as the release of poison, contracting a disease, hostile environments to determine if and how you are affected. These skills are skills that you don't directly use but can have an impact on the results of an event.
- Endurance  
  As Endurance increases so does your health. For every 2 levels in endurance you gain an additional point of health. Endurance may also have uses in resisting situations and effects during the game and in downtime 
- Training
    - As Training increases so does your stamina. FOr every 2 levels in Training you gain an additional point of stamina. Training may also have uses in other situations such as resisting the effects of hostile environments,
- Strangeness
  - This is a skill that you can not gain via normal means at character creation or during downtime. If you are exposed to, granted, bartered, or in some other way gained access to a Strange ability this skill becomes unlocked for you. At level 0 you still have the strange ability but are untrained in using it. As it levels up you will not only be able to use the ability to new ways but it will also increase your Willpower allowing more uses of the ability.
